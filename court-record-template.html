Order Sheet
  1. This matter is listed today for consideration on the point of framing of charges. The accused is present in person and has been produced from judicial custody. Learned counsel for both sides are present.
  2. The case of the prosecution, in brief, is that on 14 March 2026 a written complaint was submitted at Police Station Hazratganj, on the basis of which the aforesaid first information report came to be registered. Investigation was thereafter taken up by Sri Rajiv Mishra, Sub-Inspector.
  3. Upon completion of investigation, a report under Section 193 of the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 was submitted before this Court on 28 July 2026, along with the case diary, the seizure memo dated 14 March 2026 (Ext. Ka-3), the medical examination report (Ext. Ka-7) and the statements of eleven witnesses recorded under Section 180 thereof.
  4. This Court has heard the learned Additional Public Prosecutor and learned counsel for the accused at length, and has perused the record, the case diary and the documents filed therewith.
  5. Learned counsel for the accused has submitted that the material on record does not disclose the ingredients of the offences alleged, and that the accused has been falsely implicated on account of a prior civil dispute between the parties. The submission has been considered.
  6. At the stage of framing of charge, this Court is required only to ascertain whether the material on record discloses a grave suspicion against the accused; a meticulous appreciation of evidence is neither permissible nor called for at this stage. On a plain reading of the record, the statements of the informant and of the witnesses examined during investigation, read together with the seizure memo and the medical report, do disclose sufficient ground to proceed.
  7. The contention regarding false implication is a matter of defence which can appropriately be examined at trial after evidence is led, and does not furnish a ground for discharge at this stage.

In view of the above, this Court is satisfied that there exists sufficient ground for proceeding against the accused. Charges under Sections 115(2) and 351(3) of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 are accordingly framed against the accused Ramesh Chandra Verma. The charges have been read over and explained to the accused in Hindi, to which he has pleaded not guilty and claimed to be tried.

Put up on 02 January 2027 for recording the statements of prosecution witnesses. The accused shall remain in judicial custody. Summons and bailable warrants be issued to the witnesses listed by the prosecution.

Anil Kumar Srivastava
Additional District & Sessions Judge
Court No. 7, Lucknow
19 September 2026

Judgement

Reserved on: 11 September 2026   |   Pronounced on: 19 September 2026

  1. By this judgement, this Court proposes to dispose of Sessions Trial No. 214 of 2026, arising out of FIR No. 0142 of 2026 registered at Police Station Hazratganj, District Lucknow.
  2. The prosecution has examined nine witnesses in support of its case. The accused, in his statement recorded under Section 351 of the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023, has denied the prosecution case in its entirety and has stated that he has been falsely implicated. No evidence has been led in defence.
  3. Upon a careful appreciation of the ocular testimony of PW-1 and PW-2, which has remained consistent and has not been shaken in cross-examination, read in conjunction with the medical evidence of PW-6 and the recovery evidenced by Ext. Ka-3, this Court finds that the prosecution has established its case beyond reasonable doubt.
  4. The minor discrepancies pointed out by learned counsel for the accused in the timings deposed to by PW-4 are not such as go to the root of the matter, and do not displace the otherwise cogent and reliable evidence on record.

Verdict — Convicted

Accordingly, the accused Ramesh Chandra Verma is held guilty of the offences punishable under Sections 115(2) and 351(3) of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023, and is convicted thereunder.

Let the convict be heard on the question of sentence. Put up on 22 September 2026 for hearing on sentence. The convict shall remain in judicial custody until then.

Pronounced in open Court on this the 19th day of September, 2026.
